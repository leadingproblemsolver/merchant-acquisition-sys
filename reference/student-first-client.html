Turn work you have already done into a clear conversation with a potential customer.

1. Build your brief

Use facts you can show. Leave unknowns blank. Nothing is sent automatically.

2. Have the conversation

How to test this with one student
Ask them to enter an existing project and one real prospect. Can they explain the offer and choose an appropriate next action without your help?
Then record the actual result: message sent, reply, problem corrected, introduction, trial accepted, or no interest. A completed brief is not a customer.
