# RealX · First 5 merchant conversations

## Hard start gate
Ask RealX for authority to approach merchants as a representative and named AE/handoff rule. Without this, do genuine independent exploratory conversations and do not represent any commercial terms or make partnership offers.

## Fastest path
1. Open standalone app. Click `Load 23 existing food candidates`. Pick only five businesses that genuinely operate and are reachable.
2. Open the public profile for each, verify and record source. Unknown is acceptable; never promote directory claims to verified buyer demand.
3. Use the score to prioritize proven student relevance and a reachable promotions owner. Do not spend hours researching unproven economics.
4. The Conversational Mapper asks ONE question. Begin in person or on a genuine call: "Who handles student promotions and partnerships here?" Then ask about the buyer's actual goal and a recent example.
5. Record an ATTEMPT with a dated receipt/note only after an actual attempt; log REPLY with precise words after a reply. Record a meeting only when the merchant and RealX AE agree to a date/time.
6. Export JSON, copy the AE handoff, and get explicit AE acknowledgment. Record HANDOFF_ACCEPTED only after it actually happens.

## Exit rules
- Five approaches, no owners: improve channel or local access, not more software.
- Owners say no student demand: shift ICP or falsify hypothesis; no offer pitching.
- Interest but no meeting: diagnose missing authority or next-step friction.
- Meetings booked but AE not accepting: secure handoff SLA or stop booking more.
